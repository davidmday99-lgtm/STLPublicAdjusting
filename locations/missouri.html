Public Adjuster in Missouri

STL Public Adjusting is licensed to handle insurance claims across the state of Missouri. Whether you're in St. Louis, Kansas City, Springfield, Columbia, or anywhere in between—we can help you navigate the claims process and fight for a fair settlement.

Missouri Insurance Claim Laws

  • Prompt payment required — Missouri regulations (20 CSR 100-1.040) require insurers to acknowledge claims within 10 working days and decide within 15 working days of proof of loss
  • Right to appraisal — Most Missouri policies include an appraisal clause if you disagree on the amount of loss
  • Public adjusters are licensed — Missouri licenses public adjusters through the Department of Commerce and Insurance

Common Missouri Property Claims

  • Hail damage (especially spring/summer severe storms)
  • Wind and tornado damage
  • Ice dam and frozen pipe damage
  • Water damage from storms and flooding
  • Fire and smoke damage
