Public Adjuster in Illinois

STL Public Adjusting is licensed to handle insurance claims across Illinois, with a focus on the Metro East St. Louis area. We represent policyholders—not insurance companies—to help you get the settlement you deserve.

Illinois Insurance Claim Laws

  • Timely handling required — Illinois law (215 ILCS 5/154.6) requires insurers to acknowledge claims within 15 working days and decide within 15 working days after receiving proof of loss
  • Appraisal rights — Illinois policies typically include appraisal provisions for disputed claims
  • Public adjusters licensed — Licensed through the Illinois Department of Insurance

Common Illinois Property Claims

  • Hail and wind damage from Midwest storms
  • Tornado damage
  • Water damage (burst pipes, ice dams, flooding)
  • Fire and smoke damage
  • Sewer and drain backup

Illinois Areas We Serve
