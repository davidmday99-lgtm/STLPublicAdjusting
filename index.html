Appraisal support

If you and the carrier disagree on the amount of loss, your policy may allow appraisal. We help organize documentation, prepare estimates, and coordinate the process.

Tip: Keep your claim file organized—photos, repair estimates, receipts, correspondence, and any reports.

A public adjuster is a licensed professional who represents the policyholder (you). We help document damages, prepare estimates, and advocate for a fair settlement.
Many policies allow appraisal when there’s a dispute over the amount of loss. Each party selects an appraiser, and an umpire may be used if needed. Policy language controls the process and timelines.
